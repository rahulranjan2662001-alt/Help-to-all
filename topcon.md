# TOPCon

TOPCon (Tunnel Oxide Passivated Contact) is a crystalline-silicon cell architecture that uses a very thin oxide layer and a passivated contact to reduce recombination losses.

## Why it matters
- High efficiency potential
- Strong industrial scalability
- Compatible with established crystalline-silicon manufacturing approaches
- Increasingly important in the commercial PV market

## Key questions for benchmarking
- Commercial cell efficiency
- Module efficiency
- Wafer format
- Metallization / busbar architecture
- 0BB adoption
- Production scale
