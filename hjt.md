# HJT

HJT (heterojunction technology) combines crystalline silicon with thin-film semiconductor layers to create a heterostructure cell.

## Key characteristics
- High efficiency potential
- Strong temperature performance
- High bifacial potential
- Different manufacturing process from conventional PERC/TOPCon
